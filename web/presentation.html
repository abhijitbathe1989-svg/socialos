Revenue-first delivery policy

Pre-revenue

Use the current stack, organic discovery and manual workflows. Avoid new paid tools and ads.

First collected revenue

Cover direct costs, taxes, refunds and a cash reserve before reinvestment.

Scale with proof

Add paid integrations only when customer demand and payback are demonstrated.
