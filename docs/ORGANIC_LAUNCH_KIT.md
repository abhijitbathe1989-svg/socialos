# SocialOS Organic Launch Kit

This kit is designed for a zero-ad-spend pilot. Replace bracketed fields with a real city, service, verified provider details and a working contact path before publishing. Do not claim provider coverage where none exists.

## 1. Positioning

**For customers:** Find and contact service providers in your area through one clear enquiry flow. Check provider details and credentials independently before hiring.

**For providers:** Create a free SocialOS listing, describe the services and locations you actually cover, and receive customer enquiries through a private provider inbox.

**Trust promise:** SocialOS displays only providers who have passed its documented review process. A directory listing is not a guarantee, endorsement, insurance policy or substitute for customer due diligence.

## 2. Provider recruitment message

**Headline:** Get your service business discovered — free early listing

**Copy:**
Are you a [SERVICE] provider serving [CITY / AREA]?
SocialOS is opening its early provider directory. Create a free business profile, list the services and areas you genuinely cover, and manage customer enquiries in one dashboard.
New profiles are reviewed before public visibility. You will not be charged to register during the pilot.
Apply here: [MARKETPLACE URL]

Please list only services you are qualified and legally permitted to provide. Verification may require business registration, identity, professional licences and insurance where applicable.

## 3. Customer launch message

**Headline:** Looking for a reliable [SERVICE] in [CITY]?

**Copy:**
Tell us what you need, where the work is located and how a provider can contact you. SocialOS helps you discover reviewed local service providers and send an enquiry directly.
Compare credentials, ask for a written quote and agree scope/payment directly with the provider before work begins.
Explore: [MARKETPLACE URL]

Only publish this message after at least a small set of genuinely verified providers is live for that service and location.

## 4. Ten organic post ideas

1. **Provider call:** “Are you a licensed/qualified [SERVICE] provider in [CITY]? Join the free pilot directory.”
2. **Customer education:** “Five questions to ask before hiring a [SERVICE] provider.”
3. **Price transparency:** “What affects the cost of [SERVICE] in [CITY]? A practical quote checklist.”
4. **Safety:** “Which credentials should a customer check before hiring a [REGULATED SERVICE]?”
5. **Local need:** “What service is hardest to find in [AREA]? Tell us what local supply is missing.”
6. **Provider spotlight:** Introduce one verified provider, service area, credentials checked and customer contact route. Obtain written consent.
7. **How-to:** A seasonal maintenance checklist relevant to the selected service.
8. **Customer journey:** A short screen recording showing how to filter a category and send an enquiry; use test data only in the demo.
9. **Provider education:** “How to respond to an enquiry with a clear scope, timeline and written estimate.”
10. **Pilot feedback:** Ask early users what was confusing and what provider information they need before contacting someone.

## 5. Referral loop

- Ask every verified provider to share their public profile with existing customers and local networks.
- Ask customers after a completed service whether they want to share the directory with a neighbour; do not add them to marketing lists without separate consent.
- Give providers a simple share link and track referral source.
- Do not pay cash referral rewards until local referral, tax and regulated-sector rules are checked.
- Never fabricate reviews, enquiries, testimonials, provider credentials or completed jobs.

## 6. Weekly pilot cadence

| Day | Activity | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Interview providers and update service coverage | Provider gaps and objections |
| Tuesday | Publish one useful local guide | Search/social content |
| Wednesday | Provider recruitment and profile completion | New review submissions |
| Thursday | Customer education post and community engagement | Genuine enquiries |
| Friday | Follow up every open enquiry and review response time | Lead outcome records |
| Saturday | Provider/customer feedback and service quality review | Product fixes |
| Sunday | Funnel and unit-economics review | Continue, change or stop decision |

## 7. Channel rules

- **Google Business Profile:** Use only for a genuinely eligible business/location; do not create fake offices or duplicate profiles.
- **Search engine content:** Publish original, location-specific pages only where actual provider supply exists. Avoid doorway pages for cities with no service availability.
- **Facebook/Instagram/LinkedIn:** Use owned accounts; publish manually until platform permissions and publishing integration are implemented.
- **WhatsApp/email/SMS:** Use only permission-based contacts, identify the sender, honour opt-outs and comply with local rules. No bulk unsolicited outreach.
- **Paid search/social ads:** Not part of the free pilot. Require owner approval of channel, creative, targeting, budget, duration and hard spend cap.

## 8. Daily lead operating standard

1. Acknowledge each genuine enquiry within the published service window.
2. Confirm customer intent and the exact service/location.
3. Route only to a verified, active provider who serves that area.
4. Share customer data only under the recorded consent and privacy notice.
5. Record provider response, quote, outcome, complaint and any refund/dispute.
6. Close or delete data under the retention policy when no longer needed.

## 9. First-pilot scorecard

Record weekly:
- Number of reviewed providers and active service listings.
- Percentage of providers responding within one business day.
- Genuine enquiries, qualified enquiries and duplicate/spam enquiries.
- Quote rate and completed-service confirmation rate.
- Customer complaints, provider complaints and refunds.
- Repeat use and provider retention.
- Cash collected, direct costs and contribution margin.

Do not scale a city or service based on followers, impressions or registrations alone. Scale only when supply is real, enquiries are handled, customers report value and contribution margin is positive.
