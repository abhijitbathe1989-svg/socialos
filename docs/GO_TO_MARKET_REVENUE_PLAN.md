# SocialOS: Revenue-first launch plan

## Business objective

Build a cash-generating business with near-zero upfront spend. Prioritise paid customer validation, retention and contribution profit over feature count or vanity metrics.

## Strategic recommendation: sell the operating tool before trying to become a global portal

A new property portal starts with a cold-start problem: buyers need inventory and agents need buyer traffic. SocialOS currently has no published property listings or published service providers. Competing head-on with established portals before acquiring supply and demand would burn time and money.

The first commercial wedge should be a **property-agent lead and follow-up operating system**:
- Give independent agents and small brokerages a simple place to manage listings, enquiries, follow-ups, viewing requests and offers.
- Let agents publish verified inventory to the SocialOS marketplace when it is ready.
- Offer free owner listings to seed supply; do not promise buyer traffic before it exists.
- Sell the agent's business workflow and time saved, not guaranteed leads or sales.

## Initial customer segment and geography

Start with one manageable sales territory, not 73 simultaneous launches:
1. Pilot in Maharashtra, India, where onboarding and support can be done manually at no advertising cost.
2. Recruit 10–20 independent agents / small brokerages in one city cluster first.
3. Expand to Pune and other Indian cities only after the pilot shows repeat usage and willingness to pay.
4. Treat UAE, UK, US and Europe as later market tests; each requires local compliance, supply acquisition and support.

The initial pilot geography is a hypothesis, not a claim that demand has already been validated.

## Offer and pricing experiments

Do not activate live billing until merchant onboarding, webhook handling, subscription entitlements, refunds and support processes are tested.

Suggested India launch experiment:
- Free: one agent, basic inventory, limited CRM and manual enquiry tracking.
- Solo: ₹999/month — larger inventory, enquiry pipeline, reminders and basic performance dashboard.
- Team: ₹2,499/month — multi-user team, assignment, shared pipeline and team reporting.
- Brokerage: ₹6,999/month — multiple teams/branches, advanced reporting and admin controls.

These are test prices, not researched market averages. Validate with paid pilots and customer interviews. Offer a time-limited founding pilot only in exchange for structured feedback and actual weekly usage; avoid indefinite free service.

Additional monetisation after product-market evidence:
- Featured property placement, clearly labelled as sponsored.
- Paid service-provider profiles and lead-management tools.
- Brokerage seats / additional team members.
- Optional verified onboarding assistance where permitted.

Do not charge transaction commissions or hold deposits/escrow in the initial product. That introduces legal, payment and dispute complexity.

## Free customer acquisition system

Weekly founder-led sales:
- Build a list of 50 relevant local agents using public business contact information and referrals.
- Contact 10–15 per working day with a personalised message; respect consent and local anti-spam rules.
- Offer a 20-minute setup and import their existing listings from a spreadsheet they provide.
- Ask them to use SocialOS for real follow-ups for 14 days.
- Run a weekly review: leads entered, follow-ups completed, viewings booked, time saved and willingness to pay.
- Ask active agents for one referral after they have experienced value.
- Publish useful organic content: local listing checklists, buyer/renter guides, agent productivity tips and market explainers. Avoid unsupported price predictions.

No paid advertising, paid AI API, paid data feeds, contractors or new infrastructure subscriptions before collected revenue.

## First 30-day validation targets

These are operating targets, not forecasts or guaranteed outcomes:
- 50 qualified agent prospects identified.
- 20 discovery conversations.
- 10 agents onboarded.
- 5 agents active weekly by week four.
- At least 3 explicit paid-pilot commitments at a stated price.
- 100 legitimate property records reviewed for accuracy and permission to publish.
- Measure enquiry response time, follow-up completion, viewings requested and agent retention.

If agents will not use the workflow weekly or pay after a meaningful trial, interview lost prospects and narrow the product before adding features.

## Unit economics and cash discipline

Track monthly:
- MRR = active paid subscriptions recognised for the month.
- Gross profit = collected revenue minus payment fees, refunds, variable hosting / messaging / support costs.
- Contribution profit = gross profit minus acquisition costs directly attributable to customers.
- Net operating cash = cleared cash received minus actual cash expenses and tax obligations.

Do not count test checkouts, unsigned commitments, invoices or forecast revenue as cash. Keep a reserve for refunds, taxes and essential service costs. Add a paid service only when customer revenue covers it and expected payback is documented.

Illustrative subscription revenue before fees, tax, refunds and costs:
- 10 Solo customers × ₹999 = ₹9,990 MRR.
- 25 Solo customers × ₹999 = ₹24,975 MRR.
- 50 Solo customers × ₹999 = ₹49,950 MRR.
- 100 Solo customers × ₹999 = ₹99,900 MRR.
- 20 Team customers × ₹2,499 = ₹49,980 MRR.

These are arithmetic scenarios, not sales forecasts.

## Product priorities tied to revenue

P0 — before selling:
- Fix marketplace messaging to accurately describe global supported coverage.
- Make agent onboarding and property import easy.
- Connect enquiry capture to the agent CRM without exposing private customer data.
- Give agents a clear lead pipeline and follow-up reminders.
- Add listing verification and provider review queue.
- Instrument product usage and paid-conversion metrics.
- Finish live billing safely before collecting subscriptions.

P1 — after first active pilot:
- Saved-search alerts and viewing calendar.
- Team lead assignment and response-time reporting.
- Simple customer-facing enquiry confirmation.
- Import/export and duplicate detection.
- Featured listings with clear sponsored labels.

P2 — only after repeatable sales:
- Official listing-feed partnerships / licensed data integrations.
- AI lead qualification using low-cost or local models where feasible.
- Multi-country billing and tax handling.
- More advanced transaction workflows.

## Current product truth and launch gates

As of this plan:
- The database includes 73 enabled countries, 191 cities and 25 property-service categories.
- The marketplace has no published property listings or published service providers yet.
- Property viewing and offer workflow tables are present in the database; customer-facing forms and agent dashboard integration remain to be completed.
- The existing SocialOS subscription links are Stripe test-mode links. They do not collect real money or activate subscriptions.
- The marketplace is not connected to MLS/IDX, Zillow, Realtor.com, Bayut or Property Finder feeds. Do not scrape proprietary portals or imply their inventory is available.
- Do not claim a property, provider, licence, title, viewing slot or transaction is verified unless the relevant checks have actually been completed.

Launch gate: do not advertise SocialOS as a live revenue-producing marketplace until at least one real agent has onboarded, a real enquiry has been handled end-to-end, and the public listing and privacy controls have been tested.

## Founder dashboard: weekly decision rule

Continue a feature only if it improves at least one of:
1. Qualified agent acquisition.
2. Weekly active agent retention.
3. Enquiry-to-viewing conversion.
4. Agent response time / follow-up completion.
5. Paid conversion or contribution profit.

Otherwise defer it. The company goal is sustainable collected cash, not the largest possible feature list.
